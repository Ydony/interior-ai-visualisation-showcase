## The story

Clients struggle to picture a room from design drawings, and realistic renders take a specialist and days of waiting.

Interior AI Visualisation turns a view from the designer's own drawing into a realistic photo of the same room in minutes. Want a different cabinet colour or an extra lamp? Change just that one thing; everything else stays the same.

Clients see the finished room before anything is built, and decide faster.

## What it does

- Turns a design view into a realistic picture of the same room
- Changes one part of the room, such as cabinet colour, and keeps the rest
- Adds new items, such as pendant lamps, with matching light
- Runs on the designer's own computer, so designs stay private
- Compares picture options side by side
- Starts from the designer's drawing in one click
- Suggests a material for each surface and remembers the project
- Simple app with a picture-first workspace in three steps

## Future plans

- Painted surfaces keep their original colour
- A design library that learns from every new design
- Click an item in the picture and leave a comment
- Easy undo of a change
- Better material guesses from reference photos
- Move through the whole house and pick the view
- Product names and links for items in the picture
- More lighting moods and sharper 4K pictures
- Several rooms in one go
